About

About Me

I am a graduate student in Economic Analytics at Towson University, where I also serve as a Graduate Assistant. My interests are centered on applied economics, causal inference, health economics, public policy, econometrics, and data science.

I enjoy working with data to understand economic and policy questions, particularly when careful empirical analysis can help explain what is happening, why it matters, and what the evidence suggests.

Alongside my academic work, I serve as Vice President of the Graduate Student Association for the College of Business and Economics. I am interested in opportunities that allow me to apply economic reasoning, quantitative analysis, and data science to real-world problems.
